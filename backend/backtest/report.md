# Backtest: AirDay plans vs measured air, Delhi, Oct–Dec 2025

Generated by `uv run python -m backtest.run`. Data is committed under `backtest/data/` so anyone can rerun it.

- Period: 2025-10-01 to 2025-12-31, school days (Mon–Sat), school hours 07:00–13:00.
- Stations (9): Anand Vihar, New Delhi - DPCC, DTU, New Delhi - CPCB, Jahangirpuri, Delhi - DPCC, Lodhi Road, New Delhi - IMD, Mundka, Delhi - DPCC, North Campus, DU, Delhi - IMD, Punjabi Bagh, Delhi - DPCC, Sirifort, Delhi - CPCB, Wazirpur, Delhi - DPCC.
- Station-days scored: 708.
- Forecast: CAMS Global via Open-Meteo (archived forecasts, ~45 km, 3-hourly interpolated to hourly).
- Observed: OpenAQ public archive of CPCB/DPCC/IMD 15-minute data, averaged to hours (75% completeness).
- Bands: the production engine (`airday.rules`, `airday.engine`), worse of PM2.5 and PM10.

## Should this hour be restricted?

Hit rate = share of genuinely bad hours that AirDay flagged. False alarms = share of AirDay's warnings that turned out fine.

Observed share of school hours that were Poor or worse: 81%; Very Poor or worse: 71%.

## Decision

The forecast alone misses most bad hours: CAMS reads far cleaner than Delhi stations in winter (bias below). So the production 06:00 brief uses the latest fresh station band as a floor for the rest of the school day (`STATION_FLOOR_HOURS`, default 24). That is row 2: Very Poor+ hit rate 93% at 12% false alarms, up from 30%. We chose the floor over the ratio method because missing a bad hour costs more than an unneeded indoor PE period.

A naive persistence baseline scores about as well. AirDay's value is not a better forecast: it is turning the reading into a timetable decision, before 07:00, with a parent note and a rule ID on every line.

## Morning PE (08:00–10:00), day level, 06:00 email, forecast only

On 538 station-days the 08:00–10:00 air was Very Poor or worse. The email moved PE indoors on 256 of them (48%). Of the 261 mornings it flagged, 5 were not Very Poor (2% false alarms).

## Forecast bias

CAMS PM2.5 vs station, school hours: mean bias -102.6 µg/m³, mean absolute error 106.0 µg/m³ over 4366 hours. Negative bias means the forecast reads cleaner than reality.

## Limits

- Archived CAMS forecasts are the closest-lead forecast for each hour, so the email strategy is slightly flattered versus a forecast issued at 06:00.
- Holidays and GRAP school closures are not removed; every Mon–Sat counts as a school day.
- Stations are scored separately; nearby stations share a forecast grid cell, so results are correlated.
- The 1-hour lag for station data is an assumption until the Thursday lag measurement replaces it.
